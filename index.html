Emmanuel Abu-Ekpeshie, product designer

Hey, I’m Emmanuel 👋

I’m a product designer who enjoys turning complex problems into simple, thoughtful digital experiences.

I’m currently designing at Tinglemore. Previously, I led design at 8x Social and 8x Sales, and was part of the design team at PlateHack, an AI-powered foodtech startup, and Sabinovates, a design and development agency.

Over the past 4+ years, I’ve worked across different products and industries, figuring out how to make digital experiences more intuitive, useful, and enjoyable. I care about understanding the people behind the problems, asking the right questions, and creating products that look good but, more importantly, work well for the people using them.

Outside of design, I’m a Real Madrid fan, love the LA Lakers, and I’m curious about how technology shapes the way we live, think, and interact with the world.
